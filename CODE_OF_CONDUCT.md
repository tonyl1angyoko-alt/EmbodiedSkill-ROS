# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, appearance, ability, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven.
- Accept constructive feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Do not publish personal data, credentials, robot network details, or proprietary materials.
- Do not exaggerate Mock, simulation, ROS2, or hardware validation.
- Focus criticism on the work and its reproducibility, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior and enforcement

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly false research claims are unacceptable. Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1.
